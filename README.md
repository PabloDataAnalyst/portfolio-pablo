### 🎮 [Video Game Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/PabloDataAnalyst/videogames-analysis)

**Goal:** Identify which video game genres and companies have historically sold the most (1971–2020) to guide a company in choosing the most profitable genres and platforms for future releases.\
**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI\
**Highlights:** Data cleaning, aggregation, and interactive visualization with hover-based tooltips\
**Result:** Interactive dashboard showing the top genres and leading companies by category over five decades.

**Goal:** Identify patterns between vehicle weight and horsepower.\
**Tools:** R, tidyverse, janitor, ggimage, scales\
